What this does not repair

account, rotate exposed production credentials or determine all attacker access.
The account owner should inspect sessions, authentication methods, tokens,
connected apps, collaborators, deploy keys, webhooks, Actions access and audit
evidence; revoke unauthorized access and rotate anything exposed. This task does
not claim those account actions have been performed.
